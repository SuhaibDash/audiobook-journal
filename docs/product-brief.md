# Product brief

## Problem

Audiobook listeners can lose important story details and their own reactions,
especially when reading a long series over time. Notes are inconvenient to
capture in generic tools, and completed books can disappear from memory.

## Product statement

Audiobook Journal is a private iPhone companion that lets a listener track
their reading history and capture spoiler-safe notes, reflections, and
predictions by book and optional chapter.

## Initial user

The first user is the developer: an iPhone-owning audiobook listener who wants
to remember completed books and retain details across a series.

## Success for v1

The developer uses the app for their current audiobook, can reopen it after a
week, and can quickly recall both the book's important details and their own
thoughts before starting the next book.

## Non-goals for v1

- Playing audiobooks or integrating with audiobook providers
- Accounts, cloud sync, or social sharing
- Automatically detecting audiobook chapters
- AI summaries or recommendations
